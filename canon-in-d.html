Canon in D — on the 0700-AAM

A complete, followable tutorial. Every step uses the machine's real controls, in order. Follow it top to bottom and Canon in D will be looping on the machine, built one part at a time.

The harmony loop is eight chords, one every two steps of the 16-step loop:

D — A — Bm — F#m — G — D — G — A

Step 0 — Plug in a keyboard

A keyboard plugs into the machine (USB / MIDI in). The melodic parts below are played on the keys (or on the pads once the keygroup is set — Step 2); the machine captures them into its loop.

Step 1 — Set the tempo and program the drums

  1. Tap the tempo in on TAP TEMPO — slow and stately, about 53 BPM.
  2. Open the step sequencer and click in the drums: - Kick on steps 1, 5, 9, 13 - Snare on steps 5 and 13 - Hats on every odd step
  3. Press PLAY. The drums loop — the spine everything else hangs on.

Step 2 — Make the pads a keyboard (16 LEVEL keygroup)

  1. Strike the melodic pad (pad 9), then press 16 LEVEL. All sixteen pads now play that sound chromatically, source pitch on pad 5. On this keygroup: pad = note − 56.
  2. For the bass later: strike the bass pad (pad 13) + 16 LEVEL. On that keygroup: pad = note − 37.

Step 3 — Capture the chords (OVERDUB)

  1. Drums playing, press OVERDUB — its light comes on. With OVERDUB lit, every note you play is written into the loop.
  2. One triad per chord slot (each chord lasts two steps):
Chord Notes Pads
D D4 F#4 A4 6 · 10 · 13
A A3 C#4 E4 1 · 5 · 8
Bm B3 D4 F#4 3 · 6 · 10
F#m A3 C#4 F#4 1 · 5 · 10
G B3 D4 G4 3 · 6 · 11
D D4 F#4 A4 6 · 10 · 13
G B3 D4 G4 3 · 6 · 11
A A3 C#4 E4 1 · 5 · 8
  1. The loop comes around playing your chords back. Hands off.

Step 4 — Capture the bass

Bass keygroup (pad 13 + 16 LEVEL), OVERDUB still lit — one root per chord, on the first step of its slot:

D3 (pad 13) · A2 (pad 8) · B2 (pad 10) · F#2 (pad 5) · G2 (pad 6) · D3 (pad 13) · G2 (pad 6) · A2 (pad 8)

Step 5 — Capture the sparkle (arpeggio pings)

Melodic keygroup again. On the second step of each chord slot, strike the chord's top note once:

A4 (13) · E4 (8) · F#4 (10) · F#4 (10) · G4 (11) · A4 (13) · G4 (11) · E4 (8)

Step 6 — Let it play, then end it

Let the full loop run — drums, chords, bass and sparkle, every part played in by hand. To finish: stop the loop and strike a final D chord (D3 in the bass + D4 F#4 A4 on the melodic pads) and let it ring.

Every part in this song was played in live, one layer at a time, and looped on this machine. Simulator: https://rasrobo.github.io/0700-aam-sim/ — an independent simulator; not affiliated with Music Tribe / Behringer.
