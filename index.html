The song is the proof.

LoopProof decomposes a song into a tutorial, then proves the tutorial correct by following it in a screen recording of the simulator and producing the song — drums, chords, bass, figures and melody, every part played in live, one layer at a time, and captured into the loop of the Behringer Kick (0700-AAM).

Every recipe below comes from a verified score, is machine-checked note for note, and can be recreated by anyone in the free simulator — no hardware needed. The performances are reference runs: the simulator is operated in a screen recording by an automated performer, so every press and strike is exact and repeatable, and the sound is rendered from the performed notes.
